# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, create test plans, and validate that delivered features meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks. They bridge quality concerns between Product, Development, and Stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Own the test strategy and QA approach for the project
- Participate in acceptance criteria refinement during planning
- Design and execute test plans (unit, integration, end-to-end, security)
- Validate acceptance criteria before PRs are approved
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Escalate quality risks that could impact release readiness
- Collaborate with Developers on testability and test design

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality and acceptance standards
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Maintain high test coverage and reduce regression risk

### Typical Communication
- Planning meetings and sprint ceremonies
- Test plan documentation and QA reporting
- Acceptance criteria discussions with Product and Development
- Pre-release smoke testing coordination
- Quality dashboards and metrics reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to design testable solutions and review test coverage
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to clarify and validate acceptance criteria
- Partners with **Technical Leads** on test architecture and automation strategy
- Reports quality status to **Project Managers** for release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define system architecture, design decisions, and technical strategy. They guide the engineering team on implementation approach and technical risk mitigation while ensuring system quality and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical design and architecture decisions
- Conduct architecture and design reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Ensure code quality, maintainability, and scalability
- Participate in capacity planning and effort estimation
- Champion technical excellence and reduce technical debt

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build systems that can evolve with future requirements

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Code reviews and mentorship
- Risk and dependency escalation
- Planning and estimation sessions
- Technical documentation and design specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guides **Developers** on technical approach, design patterns, and code quality standards
- Partners with **QA Leads** on test architecture and automation frameworks
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on technical dependencies and risk mitigation

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads own user experience and usability outcomes. They collaborate with Product and Engineering to ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with design standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows and interaction design
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specs
- Conduct usability testing and incorporate feedback
- Ensure consistency with design systems and brand guidelines
- Participate in acceptance criteria to define usability standards
- Advocate for user needs in trade-off discussions
- Collaborate with Developers on design handoff and implementation

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce support escalations and user frustration
- Maintain design consistency and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Design specs and handoff to engineering
- Usability testing results and feedback
- Design review sessions with Product and Engineering
- Planning meetings to define UX acceptance criteria
- Design system documentation and guidelines

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** to understand user needs and define feature requirements
- Collaborates with **Developers** on design feasibility and implementation details
- Works with **QA Leads** to define usability acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on design timelines and dependencies

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests and provide authority for prioritization, resource allocation, and escalation decisions. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and deliver business value.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Make prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Serve as escalation point for Level 3 risks and blockers
- Communicate outcomes to broader leadership and stakeholders
- Remove organizational barriers to delivery
- Champion the project and secure necessary organizational support

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain executive alignment and resource support
- Remove organizational blockers

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval
- Monthly stakeholder reviews
- Escalation and decision requests
- Release announcements and business impact reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provides strategic direction and approval authority to **Project Managers**
- Aligns business priorities with **Product Managers** on roadmap and success metrics
- Escalates Level 3 blockers and makes executive decisions for the delivery team
- Communicates project outcomes to broader organizational leadership

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Delivery Coordinators facilitate team ceremonies, remove blockers, and maintain team rhythm and velocity. They enable smooth execution and foster a culture of continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate blockers and impediments
- Track team velocity and sprint health
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Manage project board and backlog hygiene
- Coordinate across teams and dependencies
- Remove administrative friction from the development process

### Goals
- Maintain consistent team velocity and delivery rhythm
- Remove friction from the development process
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and standups
- Blocker escalation and status updates
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Dependency coordination across teams

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Facilitates ceremonies and collaboration among all team members
- Escalates blockers to **Project Managers** and **Product Managers**
- Tracks team capacity and communicates with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on workload
- Coordinates dependencies with other Scrum Masters and **Project Managers**
